Every September, Kolding Gymnasium kicks off the school year with its annual Sports Day. It’s the one day where STX, HF, and IB students drop the books to go head-to-head in a massive, high-energy competition. While this happens annually, this year was especially noteworthy for the IB students as this year’s winning team managed to go undefeated. The Danish event has been a long-standing tradition in Kolding Gymnasium with demanding and creative activities being done all in costumes. Each class chooses a theme for the Sports day and dress accordingly. This makes clear teams and makes for fun moments as teams become natural enemies. Some of this year’s teams included military, police and robbers and men in black. The day is a natural and fun way for first year students to get to know each other and create fun memories.
 
Every teacher and school employee gather every year to provide new and interesting activities to the students, and this was true again in 2026. While the day is largely for the students it still provides fun for everyone.
The day as a whole had high tensions as every team wanted the prize. They fought for these in many fun and unique games. This included memorization games, tug of war and a multi people water relay run. Each activity required different skillsets which allows every student to participate, and the diversity of the class therefore comes in handy. Every student was eager to participate and when the day was over people left tired and happy. The day gives a great introduction to the school for newcomers and provides a break in stressful times for the older students.  
 
For many years, the IB has done poorly during the Sports day, but this was the year of change. With its leaders Óðinn Örn Kjærnested and the mighty Thomas Songwon Wallis they managed to achieve a result not seen by the IB in recent memory. While both STX and HF managed good positioning in overall rankings, none exceeded the team spirit provided by the DP2 (2) team and as the day kept going their victory became clear. As the announcement was given the group screamed in celebration. The team had now won a free dinner from the school cafeteria and the right to call themselves winners. In total the IB ended up placing twice on the podium, having won both the gold and a shared silver medal and having none of their teams finishing within the bottom 5. The day proved to be a success for the IB and something every DP2 student can no doubt look back at after they graduate.
