Every September, Kolding Gymnasium kicks off the school year with its legendary annual Sports Day. It’s the one day where STX, HF, and IB students drop the books to go head-to-head in a massive, high-energy competition. While this happens annually, this year was especially noteworthy for the IB students due to their ability to push themselves to their limits. Due to their mixed backgrounds the IB managed to dominate most of their activities with innovative thinking and problem solving from around the globe. This led to the IB placing twice on the podium, having won both the gold and silver medal and having none of the IB teams finishing within the bottom 5.
