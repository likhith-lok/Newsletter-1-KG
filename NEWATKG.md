GOODBYE, FIVE

HELLO, FOCUS
AND FLEX! 
Written by Dp2's students : 
Manida , Julia , Irina 
Welcome to a whole new way of learning! 
If you are just joining us or getting settled into the new
school year, you are in for a fresh experience. We have a
few changes in our school, some exciting new additions
to our weekly routine, which are Focus Days and Flex
Days. They were designed to give us more balance,
breathing room, and deep focus.
The new setup is pretty straightforward. On Focus Days,
instead of rushing between five different classes every
single day, we dedicate four solid hours to diving deep
into one single subject. It is an amazing way to really get
into the zone and master a topic without distractions.
On Flex Days, we get to stay home on Wednesdays to
work independently. It is a good opportunity for us
students to manage our own time, skip the commute,
and handle our assignments at our own pace.
The general vibe around school is that people really like
it! Having that mid-week break to work from home is a
massive win, and the extra time for independent study
feels like a great step forward.
STUDENTS SPEAK: WHAT'S
WORKING (AND WHAT COULD BE
BETTER)
Because this is a brand new system for
everyone, we have also been collecting
helpful feedback from students on how we
can tweak things to make it even better as
we go. While four hours of deep focus is
cool, staring at the exact same subject all
morning can get a bit heavy, so many
students are suggesting we split the day in
half to cover two different subjects instead
of just one.
"That Wednesday at
home gives me time to
actually understand
my homework instead
of just rushing it in." 
2nd year student, KG
At the same time, while Wednesdays at home
are awesome, some students would love to see
Flex Day move to Friday. That would give
everyone a longer weekend and a much bigger,
uninterrupted block of time to tackle major
assignments and really get into the flow of
working. Along with that, trying to submit work
by 09:50 can feel a bit rushed when you are
actually trying to sit down, learn, and understand
the material. Pushing deadlines to the end of the
day, around 15:00 at least, would give everyone
the breathing room to do their best work
without unnecessary stress.
This new schedule is off to a super
exciting start, and it is all about finding
the best balance for everyone. How are
you enjoying the new days so far? Let us
know for the next newsletter
