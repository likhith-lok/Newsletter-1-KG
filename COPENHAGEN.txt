Day 1 

Everyone was excited and waiting for the train to arrive at Kolding station. This was the beginning of an amazing excursion to Copenhagen. The students were divided into two groups and they were taking two different trains to Copenhagen the first one leaves at 8:15 and the other leaves at 9:00. During the 2 hour journey, students played UNO, chatted and even some of them celebrated the birth of their friend on the train. All these experiences made the journey lively and entertaining for everyone in the train.

As they arrived in Copenhagen the first thing they had to do was to leave their lugages in the basement of the hotel ' The Urban House's ' basement and they headed straight to Rådhuspladsen where they had some free time. Then they visited The Museum of illusions, where they explored an upside-down house and rooms that played tricks on their perceptions. The student commonly enjoyed an illusion which had a bridge you had to pass that felt like your head is moving and you are not moving in the straight line 

The final activity of the day was to visit the Copenhagen International Museum, where they explored historical objects and cultures from around the world. Working in groups they searched for different items from different regions and had to take the group pictures and send it to the class group. 

This was the last activity for the day and then the students were free to do whatevr they wanted to. However, they have to be back in their rooms by 10:30 which was the curfew time and if they were not available at the time of attendance they could face consequences like even being suspended from the school. 

Day 2

The second day began with an early breakfast at the hotel. The meal offered a typical danish spread of bread jam and butter although it was not everyone's first choice 

The main highlight for students interested in politics and legal structure was the visit to the Danish parliament. After passing through the securtiy check the students met Mr. benny Engelbrecht a member of parliament and former minister for transport who guided them through the parliament. He explained the importance of everything in the parliament including denmarks legal structure and politics. At the they got 15 minutes to question the Minister and it was an amazing opportunity for the students interested in politics. And they ended the tour by taking some group pictures and some students also took individual pictures with him.

Later they had to hurry to to catch a canal tour, which offered a beautiful views around copenhagen from water. It was a 45 minutes boat ride on a sunnny day provided a relaxing experience and it was the last activity planned for the day.

Day 3 

After enjoying their final breakfast at the hotel they headed to the train station. While waiting for train some bought snacks while the others simply waited for the departure. The journey back was much quiter and was replaced by sleeping passengers and low conversations. 

After arriving at kolding, everyone went their separate ways, with some heading towards buss stop and others waiting for their parents. 

It was an great opportunity to know each other, learn danish history and culture, and understand one another's backgrounds.
