This month, the entire DP2 year group went on a five-day excursion to Amsterdam. The group travelled by bus and stayed in a hostel located in the northern part of the city. Throughout the week, students followed a structured program that included visits to several museums, a guided bicycle tour, and an educational session at re-wilding forest project. These activities provided insight into Amsterdam´s culture, environmental initiatives and development.
A key component of the trip was an social and collaborative event with students from the International School of Amsterdam. This exchange offered an opportunity for communication and to strength connections between international students.
