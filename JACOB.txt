Dear Reader 
Apologies for it taking so long to get the first edition of the newsletter produced. It has taken more time than expected for a variety of reasons, but here it is at last.  
We have had another busy but happy start to the year with introductory events and activities, study trips and of course settling everybody into their new subjects and timetables. As always, it is a pleasure to see the engaged students enjoying school. 
A few headlines from me 
	· The new 4 + 1 timetable and teaching initiative is going well, as you can read later from a student perspective.
	· Kolding Gymnasium have announced they are merging with IBC, the local business college. We will eventually be moving down onto their site in Kolding’s city centre. This will not affect DP2 and DP1 students, but our current PreDP IB students may be the first to study at the new site from August 2028 if all goes as planned. I will keep you updated with more information as decisons about the merger are made.
